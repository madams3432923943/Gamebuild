Team names and player statistics are used to identify real teams and players in a game about recalling them. Team emblems in the app are original artwork and are not any team's logo.

Your account

One account per person. You are responsible for what happens under it and for keeping your password to yourself. Usernames are visible to other players, so pick one you are happy being seen with — some are refused automatically, and the game tells you when that happens.

Fair play

The ladder only means something if results are earned. Do not:

  • use scripts, bots or modified clients to play, queue, or draft;
  • arrange results with an opponent, or play yourself on a second account;
  • try to reach the database or the simulation API other than by playing the game normally;
  • flood matchmaking, friend requests, squads or chat.

Match results are calculated on the server, not in your browser, so a modified client cannot change a score. Accounts that break these rules can have results removed or be closed.

Being decent

Squad chat and usernames are shared spaces. No harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content involving minors, spam, or impersonating another player. Messages can be reported from the chat, and reported content can be removed and accounts closed.

Ending it
