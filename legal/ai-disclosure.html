AI & Simulation Disclosure

Version 1.0 — last updated August 13, 2026

In short

Every game you play in Draft Nova is invented by a computer. The players and their career statistics are real; the games, box scores and recaps are not, and never happened. AI tools were used to help write the software. No AI model is involved in playing the game, and nothing you type is sent to one.

1. Results are simulated

Every score, box score, quarter, drive, stat line, MVP, rating, draft grade and recap in Draft Nova is generated by a simulation engine. None of it is a record of a real game. No real athlete played it, and no real athlete's performance is being reported, predicted or implied.

The engine works from real historical per-season statistics, applies the game's own rules, gamestyles and tactics, and uses a seeded random number generator to decide what happens. Run the same matchup twice and you can get two different games — which is the clearest demonstration that the output is fiction rather than fact.

2. What is real, and what is not

RealGenerated by the game
Player names, the team they played for, the season, and their published per-season statistics Every simulated game and everything in it: scores, box scores, play-by-play, recaps, MVP awards
Team names, as historical fact Player ratings, gamestyle fits and draft grades — our interpretations, not official ratings

Statistics are drawn from public sources and may contain errors or gaps. See Disclaimers & Attribution for sources and for the accuracy disclaimer.

3. Do not rely on any of it

Draft Nova is entertainment. Nothing it produces is a prediction of a real sporting event or a basis for a wager, and nothing in it is betting, financial or professional advice. Simulated results are not evidence of how any athlete performed or would perform.

4. Where AI is used

In building the game: AI coding assistants were used to help write, review and document parts of the software, including parts of these legal pages. A human reviews what ships. We are telling you this because it is true, not because the code is any less ours or any less our responsibility.

In playing the game: not at all. The simulation is deterministic code — arithmetic over real statistics plus a seeded random number generator. There is no machine learning model, no large language model, and no generative AI anywhere in the match engine, the recaps, the ratings or the draft grades. The "bot" opponent in practice mode is a set of drafting rules, not an AI model.

5. Your data is not sent to an AI model

Nothing you type — your username, your squad chat, your picks — is sent to any third-party AI service, and none of it is used to train any model. If that ever changes, we will update this page and say so in the game before it takes effect, and where consent is required we will ask for it first.

6. Automated moderation

Usernames, squad names and chat messages are checked automatically against a list of prohibited terms. That is pattern matching, not AI, and it makes mistakes in both directions. You can ask a human to review any block or account action — see the Community Guidelines.
