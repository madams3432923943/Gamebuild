Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — last updated August 13, 2026

In short

We collect the minimum a game with accounts needs: an email address so you can get back in, a username other players see, and your game history. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking pixels. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never have. You can delete your account and everything attached to it from the Profile screen, or by emailing us. This summary is a convenience, not a substitute for the policy below.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Draft Nova operates the Draft Nova game and website (the "Service") and is the controller of the personal data described here. For any privacy question or request, email maxwell@gurrbrothers.com.

3. Storage in your browser

The Service does not use advertising or analytics cookies, so there is no consent banner to click through. What it does store in your browser is strictly necessary to make the game work:

  • Your sign-in session, stored by our authentication provider so you stay signed in between visits.
  • A random client identifier, so the online-player count doesn't count one person twice.
  • Your last-selected era, so the game opens where you left it.

Clearing your browser storage removes all three and signs you out.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK users)

  • Performance of a contract — running your account, matchmaking, squads, and keeping your record. Without this data there is no game to provide.
  • Legitimate interests — security, anti-cheat, rate limiting, moderation, and keeping the Service running. We have weighed these against your rights and consider them proportionate, because they are limited to what operating a multiplayer game requires.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with the law.
  • Consent — for anything optional we introduce later. We will ask, and you will be able to withdraw it.

6. International transfers

Our providers are based in the United States and may process your data there. Where data is transferred out of the EEA or UK, that transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism operated by the provider.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data — for as long as your account exists.
  • After deletion — removed from live systems immediately; residual copies in encrypted provider backups expire within 30 days.
  • Squad chat — the feature is currently switched off. Messages sent while it was available are retained, are not shown to anyone, and carry your identity only until your account is deleted. If chat returns, this policy will be updated before it does.
  • Moderation and security records — kept for up to 12 months so that repeat abuse can be recognised, then deleted.
  • Technical logs — retained by our providers on their own schedules, typically days to a few weeks.

8. Your rights, and how to use them

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Delete your account. Profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, username, records, ranked rating, badges, banners, presence, friendships and squad membership, and anonymises your squad chat messages. It cannot be undone. If you cannot sign in, email us instead and we will complete the deletion within 30 days of verifying that the account is yours.
  • Get a copy of your data. Email us and we will send you a machine-readable export within 30 days.
  • Correct your data. Username and email are editable from the Profile screen; email us about anything else.
  • Object or restrict. Email us and we will consider it and reply.
  • Withdraw consent, where processing was based on consent, without affecting what happened before you withdrew it.
  • Complain. EEA and UK users may complain to their local data protection authority. We would rather you told us first.

We do not charge for these requests and we will not treat you worse for making one. Requests go to maxwell@gurrbrothers.com.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under that age has an account, email maxwell@gurrbrothers.com and we will delete the account and its data promptly. Users in the EEA and UK under 16 need a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Security

Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never stored or visible to us in readable form. Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access to data is enforced at the database with row-level security rather than only in the app, so a modified client cannot read another player's account, and competitive results are computed on the server rather than accepted from the client.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal data we will notify you and the relevant regulator where the law requires it. If you find a vulnerability, please report it to maxwell@gurrbrothers.com rather than disclosing it publicly; we will not pursue good-faith security research.

11. Automated decision-making

Ranked rating, matchmaking and automated content filtering are algorithmic. None of them produce legal or similarly significant effects about you within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. If an automated filter blocks something you posted, or an account action is taken against you, you can ask a human to review it by emailing us.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the version and date above and give notice in the game before the change takes effect.
